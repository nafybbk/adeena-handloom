Our Story

Rooted in Barabanki Craft

Adeena Handloom is a manufacturer of premium stoles and dupattas based in Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh — a region with a long-standing handloom tradition. Our work is carried out by skilled local artisans using time-tested weaving and dyeing techniques.

Every batch goes through strict quality control before dispatch, and we produce in small batches to maintain consistency across wholesale and boutique orders. We supply to retailers, boutiques and individual buyers across India, with custom branding available for bulk orders.

— Adeena Handloom, Barabanki
