Our Story

Where Craft Becomes Legacy

Each piece in the Adeena Handloom collection is born from the hands of skilled artisans in Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh — where the tradition of handloom weaving has been alive for generations. We believe fashion should carry a soul. A story. A heartbeat.

From fine khaddar stoles to sheer chanderi dupattas, every thread is chosen with intention, every weave is an act of love.

— Adeena Handloom
